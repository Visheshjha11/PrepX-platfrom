We're on a mission to transform how educational institutions conduct online examinations, making testing more accessible, secure, and efficient for everyone.

Our Story

Prepx was born from a simple yet powerful vision: to democratize quality education through innovative online testing solutions. Founded by a team of educators and technologists, we understand the challenges that institutions face in conducting secure, reliable online examinations.

Today, we serve thousands of educational institutions worldwide, helping them create seamless testing experiences that empower both educators and students.

Our Mission

To provide educational institutions with the most advanced, secure, and user-friendly online testing platform, enabling them to focus on what matters most: delivering quality education and fostering student success.
