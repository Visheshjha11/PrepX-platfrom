Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about Prepx and our services.

How can I get exam-specific study materials?

Browse our exam categories or contact us with your specific exam name. We provide tailored resources for most competitive exams including JEE, NEET, SAT, GRE, GMAT, and more.

Is there technical support available?

Yes! Our technical team is available to help with website issues, login problems, platform navigation, and any other technical difficulties you may encounter.

Can I suggest new features or exams?

Absolutely! We value user feedback and actively work on improving our platform. Use the contact form to suggest new features, exams, or improvements.

How quickly will I receive a response?

We typically respond within 2-4 hours during business hours (9 AM - 6 PM EST), and within 24 hours for all other times. Urgent technical issues are prioritized.
