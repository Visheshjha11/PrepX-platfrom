Exam Instructions

General Guidelines

  • Read all questions carefully before answering
  • Manage your time effectively across all sections
  • Ensure stable internet connection throughout the exam
  • Do not refresh the browser during the exam

Technical Requirements

  • Use a desktop or laptop computer (mobile devices not recommended)
  • Enable JavaScript and disable pop-up blockers
  • Close all unnecessary browser tabs and applications
  • Ensure your device is fully charged or plugged in

Exam Rules

  • No external resources or materials allowed
  • No communication with others during the exam
  • Camera and microphone monitoring may be active
  • Any suspicious activity will be flagged for review

Important

Once you begin the exam, the timer will start and cannot be paused. Make sure you are ready before clicking "Begin Exam".
